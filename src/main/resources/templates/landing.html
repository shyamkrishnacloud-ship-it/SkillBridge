How It Works

Exchange skills in 4 simple steps

From registration to your first completed swap — the whole process is seamless and built around you.

Start Now
1

Create Your Profile

Sign up and fill in your department, semester and bio. Add the skills you can teach and the skills you want to learn.

2

Find Your Match

Browse smart matches scored by skill compatibility. Filter by department, availability and skill category to refine results.

3

Send a Swap Request

Choose a skill you'll teach and a skill you want to learn. Add a personal message and send your swap request.

4

Learn, Teach & Review

Complete the swap and leave a review for your partner. Build your reputation and reputation grows with every exchange.
